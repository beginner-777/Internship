Plain-words explainer for the track thread
A backend is the part of a website that receives and processes information
behind the interface. In my portfolio, the contact form is the one dynamic
feature. When a visitor completes the form, the browser first checks that the
required fields are valid. JavaScript then packages the form values and sends
them in a POST request to the FormSubmit backend. FormSubmit processes the
request and forwards the name, email, project type, and message to my email
inbox. It returns a response to the browser, and the portfolio then shows a
success or error message. This creates a complete data flow from the visitor,
through the backend, to my inbox.
